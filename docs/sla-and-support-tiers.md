# TickerPro Support Tiers & Service Level Agreements (SLA)

As a commercial provider of enterprise WhatsApp CRM solutions, building trust through guaranteed uptime and rapid support response is critical, especially for SMBs and e-commerce brands who rely on WhatsApp for direct revenue.

Below are our official Support Tiers and SLA commitments for our Managed SaaS and Enterprise Self-Hosted customers.

---

## 1. Managed Cloud Tiers (SaaS)

These tiers apply to instances hosted and managed by the TickerPro team.

| Feature | Starter (₹1,500/mo) | Growth (₹3,500/mo) | Scale (₹8,000/mo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platform Uptime SLA** | 99.0% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| **Support Channels** | Email Only | Email & Chat | Priority Slack Connect / WhatsApp |
| **Response Time (Business Hours)** | < 24 Hours | < 4 Hours | < 1 Hour |
| **Response Time (Weekend/Off-hours)** | Best Effort | Best Effort | < 4 Hours |
| **Meta Green Tick Assistance** | Self-Serve | Assisted (1 retry) | Fully Managed |
| **Dedicated Account Manager** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Custom Integration Support** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ (Up to 5hrs/mo) |

### SLA Credits (Scale Tier Only)
If we fail to meet the 99.99% uptime guarantee in a given billing month, customers on the Scale tier are eligible for service credits:
- 99.0% - 99.99% Uptime: 10% credit of monthly fee
- 95.0% - 99.0% Uptime: 25% credit of monthly fee
- < 95.0% Uptime: 50% credit of monthly fee

## 2. Enterprise Self-Hosted (Open-Core)

For BFSI, Healthcare, and large agencies deploying the TickerPro Docker image in their own VPCs (including Local LLM deployment).

**Annual Support Contract (Custom Pricing based on node count)**

- **Architecture Review:** 2 hours of deployment architecture review with our core engineering team.
- **Priority Bug Fixes:** Critical vulnerabilities or blocking bugs reported will be patched within 48 hours.
- **Support Channels:** Shared Slack Connect channel directly with TickerPro engineers.
- **Updates:** Managed database migration scripts and priority upgrade paths for new major versions.

> Note: We do *not* provide SLA guarantees for platform uptime on self-hosted instances, as we do not control the underlying infrastructure. Our SLA applies strictly to support response times and patch delivery.

---

## 3. Scope of Support

**What is Covered:**
- Platform bugs and unexpected behavior in core TickerPro features.
- Assistance with Meta Business Manager onboarding issues (Growth tier and above).
- Billing discrepancies and reconciliation.
- Best practices for template approvals to avoid Meta rejections.

**What is NOT Covered (Requires Professional Services Contract):**
- Custom Shopify theme integration or custom webhook payloads for internal ERPs.
- Designing and copywriting WhatsApp Drip Campaigns or Chatbot Flows on behalf of the client.
- Debugging issues within the customer's own AWS/GCP infrastructure (for self-hosted clients).
